### Dataset Summary

This dataset contains the questions from BrokenArXiv June 2026 used for the MathArena Leaderboard.

### Data Fields

The dataset contains the following fields:

- `problem_idx` (`int64`): Problem index within the corresponding MathArena benchmark.
- `points` (`int64`): Maximum score for the problem.
- `problem` (`string`): False mathematical statement that models are asked to prove.
- `original_problem` (`string`): Original unperturbed mathematical statement.
- `source` (`string`): arXiv identifier for the source paper.
- `title` (`string`): Title of the source arXiv paper.
- `authors` (`string`): Authors of the source arXiv paper.
